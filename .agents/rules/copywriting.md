# Copywriting & Writing Style Rules

## Strict Rule: No Long Em Dashes
- **Never use long em dashes (`—` or `&mdash;`)** in any user-facing text, markdown, HTML, metadata, or documentation.
- **Rationale**: Long em dashes frequently read as an AI-generated stylistic artifact. Content must sound completely authentic, human, and natural.
- **Alternatives**: Use commas, periods, semicolons, colons, or natural connector words (such as "while", "along with", "and").

## Brand Positioning Guidelines
- **Identity Triad**: Always sequence identity as **Ecosystem Builder, Rotaractor & Entrepreneur** (in this exact order).
- **No Corporate Titles / Fluff**: Avoid generic corporate designations or soft terms like "leader".
- **No Company Mentions**: Keep positioning timeless and thought-leader level; avoid mentioning specific corporate employer names.
- **Executive Verbs**: Use high-level verbs (e.g., *orchestrating*, *architecting*, *fostering*) over operational verbs (such as *driving*).
